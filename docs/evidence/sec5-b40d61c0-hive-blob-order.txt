3. Mechanism
================================================================================

After 68c2f376 no single step blocks for long, but one of B's transactions
reaches A only through five steps that each need one of the two session
threads free: B announces; A asks (GetPooledTransactions); B serves the eth/72
wrapper, deriving 128 cell proofs per blob; A asks for the cells (GetCells, a
blocking pump action); A verifies and admits. Both threads were busy with KZG
work for A's 5-blob transactions, one blob or one transaction at a time:

(a) A derived each 5-blob wrapper's 640 cell proofs only when B asked for it,
    on A's session thread: 146-167 ms unloaded, 601-931 ms loaded. B asks
    for each of A's transactions as it is announced, so during steps 3 and 4
    A's thread reads nothing else, including B's announcements and replies.
(b) B fetched each of A's transactions with a blocking GetCells action,
    waiting for A (up to 1,753 ms loaded) and then verifying five blobs'
    cell proofs and deriving five blob proofs one after another: 212-225 ms
    unloaded, 283-1,497 ms loaded. B's own announcements go first only
    between actions (68c2f376's broadcast-first rule), so they waited for the
    action in progress.
(c) Answering A's GetPooledTransactions for several of B's 1-blob wrappers
    derived them one after another: 268 ms for two unloaded, 2,251 ms for
    five loaded.

A loaded base run (the first of the three failing runs; ms from the start):

    3836 B GetCells for A's 2nd transaction started; A's thread is deriving
         wrappers for B's requests (784 and 931 ms, ending 4569 and 5503)
    4286 B admits its first transaction by RPC (its fifth at 5179)
    5589 B GetCells round trip done in 1,753 ms
    6659 B verify+derive of that 5-blob sidecar done in 1,070 ms
    6686 B announces its five transactions; A asks at once
    7291 A getPayloadV3: 5 blobs, A's nonce 0 only
    8941 B answers A's request for its five 1-blob wrappers after 2,251 ms
    10333 A admits B's first transaction

4. Fix (d821ef54)
================================================================================

- src/application/services/txpool-blob-admission.lisp: TXPOOL-VERIFY-BLOB-
  SIDECAR splits the sidecar into one-blob parts and runs each part's field
  checks, proof verification and blob-proof derivation through a MAP-FUNCTION
  (default MAPCAR). A derivation that fails after verification is still not
  the sender's fault (it is signalled outside the sidecar-fault handler, as
  before).
- src/app/cli/devnet/peer-sync.lisp: DEVNET-PARALLEL-MAP (at most 8 at once,
  helpers get the caller's KZG verifier specials, the first failing item's
  condition is signalled again in the caller); the devnet backend passes it
  to blob admission, and E1's DEVNET-NODE-DERIVE-BLOB-CELLS now uses it.
  DEVNET-NODE-PREPARE-POOLED-BLOB-SIDECARS derives every blob of a
  PooledTransactions reply in one parallel pass. DEVNET-NODE-WARM-POOLED-
  BLOB-SIDECARS starts that derivation on a background thread when a blob
  transaction is announced to an eth/72 peer, once per transaction, as
  pinned geth computes cells when a transaction enters its pool
  (core/txpool/blobpool newBlobTxForPool, references/go-ethereum).
- src/app/cli/devnet/types.lisp: the blob cell cache is single-flight: a
  caller that finds a blob being derived waits for that derivation (up to
  10 s) instead of deriving it again.
- src/networking/eth-sync: two optional backend callbacks,
  PREPARE-POOLED-BLOB-SIDECARS (called once per PooledTransactions reply
  before its wrappers are read) and WARM-POOLED-BLOB-SIDECARS (called after
  an announcement, must return at once).

Tests (tests/eth-gossip-tests.lisp, integration, through the devnet node's
production backend):
  ETH-72-RECEIVED-BLOB-SIDECAR-IS-VERIFIED-BLOB-BY-BLOB-IN-PARALLEL: a 5-blob
    cell sidecar is admitted, each blob verified and derived once, more than
    one at a time.
  ETH-72-POOLED-TRANSACTIONS-REPLY-DERIVES-ALL-ITS-BLOBS-AT-ONCE: a reply for
    three 1-blob wrappers derives each blob once, more than one at a time.
  ETH-72-ANNOUNCED-BLOB-TRANSACTION-IS-DERIVED-BEFORE-ITS-PEER-ASKS: after an
    announcement, with no request, the cache fills with all five blobs;
    nothing is derived on the announcing thread; the wrapper served then
    derives nothing more.
  ETH-72-REQUEST-DURING-A-WARM-UP-WAITS-FOR-IT: a request right after the
    announcement derives each of five blobs once in all.
ETH-72-BLOB-ANNOUNCEMENT-DERIVES-NO-CELL-PROOF (E1) now counts derivations
on the announcing thread, since the warm-up derives in the background.
