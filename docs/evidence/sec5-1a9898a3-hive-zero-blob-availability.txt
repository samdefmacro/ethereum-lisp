Repair

The eth/72 omitted-payload validator now accepts the vacuous zero-blob wrapper
while retaining exact commitment, transaction-blob-count, cell-proof-count,
and versioned-hash checks. A zero-commitment wrapper bypasses GetCells and is
offered to normal transaction admission, which remains responsible for its
semantic validity. Announcement-size validation also preserves the decoded
sidecar version; otherwise the empty version-1 wrapper was reconstructed as a
version-0 wrapper and differed by one encoded byte.
