Behavioral contract

GetAccountRange starts at the first available secure-trie key at or beyond the
origin. The upper bound is inclusive in geth-compatible serving behavior: when
the requested limit lies between available keys, the first available key at or
beyond that limit is included. Pinned geth also exercises origin=limit=zero,
origin equal to the first key, and origin one greater than the first key.
