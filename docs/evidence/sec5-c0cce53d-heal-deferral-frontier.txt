Section 5 healer dependency-deferral release

Revision

c0cce53d284c593bf966a9962c7d68a1a0bb7f00
fix(snap): defer proved subtrees regardless of frontier

Defect

Final TrieNodes healing decides, per proved account-subtree candidate, between
two branches. Deferring replaces the popped candidate with the at most
sixty-four storage roots its dependency proof names and skips the whole account
walk. Declining re-reads the node and expands every descendant instead.

Both production call sites gated deferral on the live frontier:

    (<= (+ stack-count missing-count deferred-storage-count remote-work-count
           (length dependencies))
        +snap-sync-heal-live-frontier-max-works+)

Declining costs far more frontier than the at most sixty-four works the gate
withheld, so the gate grew the quantity it was meant to bound and then stayed
false because it had grown. The adjacent
+snap-sync-heal-deferred-storage-target+ check is the bound that actually
applies to this branch; the traversal loop already stops on the same value.

Fix

snap-sync-heal-defer-dependencies-p takes only the deferred-storage count and
the dependency count. Both call sites call it. The live frontier no longer
participates in the decision.

Scope

This is a tested repair of a local decision plus its two production call sites.
It is not live completion evidence. Section 5 still requires a fresh-datadir
Hoodi run on this revision recording peer.snap.target_completed, healer
completion with an empty frontier, eth_syncing=false, and canonical execution
through the consensus-authorized target, followed by the shadow and validator
soak gates.

Unchanged by this revision

The traversal loop at the local (non-refill) call site still does not apply
+snap-sync-heal-live-frontier-max-works+; only the bounded-refill path does.
That asymmetry is why the frontier could exceed the cap in the first place. It
is left alone deliberately: with deferral restored the frontier has a draining
branch again, and capping a purely local traversal that has nothing to fetch
would stall it rather than bound it. Live behaviour on this revision decides
whether a separate bound is still needed.
