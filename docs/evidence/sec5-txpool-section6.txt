================================================================================
1. What was wrong at a07c912e
================================================================================

(a) A blob transaction and its sidecar entered the node through two callbacks.
    Gossip (src/networking/eth-sync/gossip.lisp ETH-ACCEPT-TRANSACTIONS)
    verified the sidecar, stored it (:ACCEPT-BLOB-SIDECAR ->
    ENGINE-PAYLOAD-STORE-PUT-BLOB-SIDECAR under the store guard), then offered
    the transaction to the pool (:ACCEPT-TRANSACTION); eth_sendRawTransaction
    verified the sidecar, admitted the transaction, then stored the sidecar.
    Consequences, each reproduced by a test:
      - a transaction the pool refuses (sender cannot pay, underpriced, ...)
        left its blobs in the blob store with no pooled owner;
      - every sidecar was KZG-verified twice, and on the gossip path the
        second verification ran UNDER the node store guard, which also
        serializes every Engine request (measured: 2 verifications, 1 under
        the guard, per one-blob gossip admission; 2 per RPC admission);
      - the blob store is a bounded cache (512 entries, 64 MiB, 3 h age;
        src/storage/chain-store/service/cache.lisp) that knew nothing of the
        pool: count, byte or age pressure evicted a POOLED transaction's blobs
        while the transaction stayed pending, unbuildable and unservable;
      - when a blob transaction left the pool (included, replaced, evicted)
        its blobs stayed until the cache's own 3 h age;
      - no pool-level blob data cap, and no TTL for blob transactions unless
        --txpool.lifetime was set (default off);
      - admission times lived only in memory: the durable txpool record was
        [subpool, encoding], so every restart gave every parked transaction
        no admission time at all, and --txpool.lifetime never expired it.

(b) EIP-7702: every admission asked "is this sender (and each authority of a
    new set-code transaction) named by a pending authorization?" by walking
    the whole pool and recovering (secp256k1 ecrecover) every authorization of
    every pooled set-code transaction (TXPOOL-AUTHORITY-RESERVED-P). Measured:
    one ordinary admission against 8 pooled set-code transactions performed 8
    recoveries; the cost is O(pool set-code authorizations) per admission,
    under the guard.

(c) Full-pool eviction ranked by the raw tip cap
    (ENGINE-PENDING-TXPOOL-CHEAPEST-TRANSACTION), so a transaction whose fee
    cap leaves 1 wei of tip at the child base fee but that advertises a tip
    cap of 90 survived, and a newcomer paying 3 was refused as underpriced
    against one paying 5.

3. Fix
================================================================================

(a) One admission path: src/application/services/txpool-blob-admission.lisp
    TXPOOL-ADMIT-BLOB-TRANSACTION runs
      1. the pool's policy and capacity checks (TXPOOL-CHECK-BLOB-ADMISSION:
         sender, nonce, balance, fees, price limit, 7702 reservations, the
         fork's blob count limit, replacement bump, and a pool cap of
         +TXPOOL-MAX-POOLED-BLOBS+ = 1024 distinct blobs, 128 MiB of blob
         data) -- reads the store, changes nothing;
      2. the sidecar's shape without curve arithmetic (counts, element sizes,
         each commitment's versioned hash);
      3. KZG verification of every proof, and for a cell-proof sidecar the
         EIP-4844 blob proof getBlobsV1 serves -- touches no store;
      4. step 1 again, then ONE CHAIN-STORE-ATOMIC-COMMIT that expires stale
         blob transactions, puts the transaction in the blob subpool and its
         blobs in the blob cache marked pool-owned. Any refusal rolls all of
         it back.
    CALL-WITH-STORE lets the devnet run steps 1 and 4 under the store guard
    and steps 2-3 outside it. A malformed or unproven sidecar signals
    TXPOOL-INVALID-BLOB-SIDECAR (the sender's fault); other refusals signal
    BLOCK-VALIDATION-ERROR. The no-sidecar entry points
    (TXPOOL-ADMIT-TRANSACTION(S)) now refuse a blob transaction outright, so
    the split cannot come back through them.
    Callers: eth_sendRawTransaction (src/api/public/transactions/
    send-raw-transaction.lisp); the eth serve backend's new
    :ACCEPT-BLOB-TRANSACTION (TRANSACTION SIDECAR) callback, which replaces
    :ACCEPT-BLOB-SIDECAR (src/networking/eth-sync/serve.lisp, gossip.lisp;
    devnet wiring in src/app/cli/devnet/peer-sync.lisp). The eth layer maps a
    sidecar fault to a protocol error (peer dropped, as before) and a pool
    refusal to a skipped entry. At most +ETH-MAX-BLOB-ADMISSIONS-PER-BATCH+ =
    16 sidecars per received batch go through KZG; the rest stay unadmitted
    and unremembered.

(b) Sidecar ownership: the txpool index keeps a refcount per versioned hash
    of every pooled transaction (src/storage/txpool/index/lookups.lisp,
    maintained at every insert/remove site of a subpool table, journaled so
    a rolled-back admission restores it). The node store answers the new
    generic CHAIN-STORE-BLOB-SIDECAR-PINNED-P from it, and the blob cache's
    bound enforcement (ENGINE-PAYLOAD-STORE-ENFORCE-CACHE-BOUNDS) never ages
    out, evicts or counts a pinned blob. A pool-owned blob (new metadata flag
    POOL-OWNED-P) is removed as soon as no pooled transaction references it,
    unless an inclusion height made it block data. Blob transactions expire
    +TXPOOL-BLOB-TRANSACTION-LIFETIME-SECONDS+ = 3 h after admission
    (ENGINE-PAYLOAD-STORE-REMOVE-EXPIRED-BLOB-TRANSACTIONS, run at every blob
    admission), local ones included.

(c) Persisted admission age: the durable txpool record is now
    [subpool, encoding, admitted-at] when the time is known
    (src/storage/node-store/persistence/export/txpool.lisp, both the full
    exporter and the forkchoice record exporter in export/orchestrator.lisp);
    the importer accepts 2 or 3 fields and restores the time. Records written
    before this change still import (no time, as before).

(d) EIP-7702 authority index: the same lookups file keeps authority ->
    {transaction} and transaction -> authorities, recovered once when a
    set-code transaction enters a subpool table and never again on removal;
    TXPOOL-AUTHORITY-RESERVED-P is one table read. Reservation semantics are
    unchanged (all subpools, pinned by a test that passes before and after).

(e) Eviction by effective tip: ENGINE-PENDING-TXPOOL-CHEAPER-P ranks by
    effective tip at the child base fee, then fee cap, then tip cap (geth
    priceHeap.cmp); a newcomer must rank strictly above the victim. Admission
    computes the child base fee (TXPOOL-CHILD-BASE-FEE) and passes it through
    the put functions' new :BASE-FEE key. TRANSACTION-EFFECTIVE-TIP (payload
    ordering) now delegates to the same index function.

Not verified

- Not deployed, not run live; no real peer has sent us a blob transaction
  through the new path.
- No cold-all (coordinator). EEST blockchain fixtures were not rerun: no
  execution, root or encoding code changed.
- Durable blob records are still never swept: a pooled blob exported to
  RocksDB stays on disk after its transaction leaves (unchanged; "offline
  rebuild is the compaction path"). Pool-owned marks are not persisted: after
  a restart, restored pooled blobs are pinned while their transactions stay
  and then fall back to the cache's ordinary three-hour age.
- A reorg that reinserts a displaced blob transaction
  (ENGINE-PAYLOAD-STORE-REINSERT-DISPLACED-BLOCK-TRANSACTIONS) still needs
  its blobs in the cache; geth keeps included blobs in a limbo until
  finality. Not examined here.
- Promotion paths that re-put transactions (queued->pending, basefee
  promotion) pass no child base fee, so their capacity eviction ranks by the
  tip capped by the fee cap (base fee 0). The admission paths pass it.
- The blob pool's capacity policy is refuse-when-full (1024 blobs), not
  geth's fee-jump eviction; the blob TTL (3 h) has no geth counterpart.
- Measured with stubbed KZG verifiers and one-blob transactions only; real
  c-kzg timing for 6-blob EIP-7594 sidecars was not measured here.
