Root cause

The retained simulator testchain at geth 101035a1 contains osakaTime=180 in
genesis.json, but its forkenv.json ends at HIVE_PRAGUE_TIMESTAMP=120 and omits
HIVE_OSAKA_TIMESTAMP. tools/hive/mapper.jq replaced the complete source config
with environment-derived fields, thereby discarding the supported Osaka
activation. The test tool folded timestamps 60, 120, and 180 into EIP-2124;
the client folded only 60 and 120. Applying the canonical CRC32 fold for the
missing eight-byte big-endian timestamp 180 to 0x9736aeb1 produces exactly
0x15e3c946, proving the failure boundary.
