Section 5 completed SNAP scheduling release

Revision

bb9cf83d7c6d482f6aa6bf4afaea63e4f62f0f99
fix(sync): ignore completed durable SNAP target

Defect

`devnet-node-durable-snap-pivot-number` treated every persisted SNAP state or skeleton record as unfinished. A completed state session and its matching skeleton therefore continued to make `devnet-node-snap-target-required-p` true after the completed target had become executable. On restart, later short Engine targets could be routed back through SNAP instead of ordinary bounded synchronization.

Invariant

- Incomplete state work remains pinned.
- A completed state session remains pinned until its exact target is executable.
- Once that target's state is available, a matching skeleton (`last-hash == target-hash`) is historical residue and neither record forces subsequent targets through SNAP scheduling.
- A mismatching skeleton remains active.
- Forkchoice publication authority remains separate; this change does not delete or release retained forkchoice targets.
