Section 9: mainnet proof-of-stake headers were held to the Ethash rules

Status
  Fixed on branch mainnet-merge-transition, commit b6c095a1 ("consensus: the
  Merge follows total difficulty, and the chain store keeps it"), base
  3c0cfeab. Mainnet stays explicitly experimental; the inventory for the
  slice is docs/gap-analysis/mainnet-inventory.md.

Mechanism
  Header validation (validate-block-header-against-config) asked
  CHAIN-CONFIG-POST-MERGE-P, a function of the configuration and the block
  number alone, whether a header is past the Merge. That function answers
  from the netsplit block when there is one, and otherwise from the TTD
  metadata: TTD-passed, or a TTD of zero or none, means post-Merge. A
  configuration with a positive TTD, no netsplit block and no TTD-passed flag
  therefore got NIL at every height. That is the mainnet preset
  (src/protocol/genesis/presets.lisp: TTD 58,750,000,000,000,000,000,000,
  no merge-netsplit-block), as in go-ethereum v1.17.6 params/config.go
  MainnetChainConfig. Every mainnet proof-of-stake header was then held to
  validate-ethash-header, whose expected difficulty for a child of a
  zero-difficulty parent at those heights is 2^54 + 131,072, and refused:

    Invalid Ethash difficulty: have 0, want 18014398509613056

  Sepolia names a netsplit block (1735371) that postdates its merge, so its
  proof-of-stake blocks below 1735371 failed the same way. Hoodi (netsplit 0)
  and Holesky (TTD 0) were never affected.
  Two authority checks asked the same function: local-dev publication
  (block-import-authorize-publication) and debug_setHead treat a
  proof-of-stake block as needing Engine authority, and under such a
  configuration they saw every block as proof-of-work.
  Why it was not seen: every public-network run so far is Hoodi, every EEST
  gate configures its forks without a positive TTD, and the one test that
  combined a positive TTD with proof-of-stake headers
  (CONFIGURED-POST-MERGE-STATUS-DOES-NOT-TRUST-HEADER-DIFFICULTY) asserted
  the refusal as intended behaviour. The chain kept no total difficulty, so
  EIP-3675's rule could not be applied even had it been wanted.
  go-ethereum v1.17.6 decides by the header's difficulty
  (consensus/beacon/consensus.go VerifyHeader: difficulty zero is
  proof-of-stake, and a positive difficulty after a zero one is refused) and
  no longer keeps total difficulty (core/rawdb/schema.go headerTDSuffix,
  "deprecated").

  and the two updated tests:

  PUBLIC-PRESET-POST-MERGE-HEADERS-VALIDATE-AS-PROOF-OF-STAKE failed with the

  MERGE-TRANSITION-FOLLOWS-THE-TERMINAL-TOTAL-DIFFICULTY and
  MERGE-TRANSITION-WITHOUT-TOTAL-DIFFICULTY-FOLLOWS-THE-PARENT with "Invalid
  Ethash difficulty: have 0, want 131072"; MERGE-TRANSITION-REFUSES-A-

  accepted; POST-MERGE-AUTHORITY-READS-THE-BLOCK-UNDER-A-TTD-CONFIGURATION
  because local-dev publication of a proof-of-stake block succeeded without
  authorization; TOTAL-DIFFICULTY-SURVIVES-EXPORT-REOPEN-AND-DIRECT-READS on
  the missing record kind; CONFIGURED-POST-MERGE-STATUS on the new keyword.

Fix (b6c095a1)
  - The chain store records each block's cumulative difficulty when it is
    put, journaled, and every export writes it as an immutable
    :total-difficulty record (prefix 0x1f, the RLP of the integer). The
    direct RocksDB provider point-reads it and drops its overlay entry with
    the block's; a full import restores the records and derives the rest in
    height order.
  - CHAIN-CONFIG-MERGE-BY-TOTAL-DIFFICULTY-P names the configurations that
    leave the transition to the chain; BLOCK-HEADER-MERGE-RULES-P applies
    EIP-3675 with the parent's total (below the TTD: proof-of-work, and a
    zero difficulty is refused; at or above: the parent must be terminal and
    the child proof-of-stake). Without a total, a proof-of-stake parent makes
    the child proof-of-stake (the geth rule); a proof-of-stake child of a
    proof-of-work parent is refused as unverifiable.
  - Candidate admission and Engine newPayload pass the parent's total; the
    two authority checks read the admitted block's own difficulty
    (BLOCK-HEADER-POST-MERGE-BLOCK-P).
  - Two tests that pinned the old answer changed: the pre-Merge
    debug_setHead fixture now has proof-of-work difficulties, and the
    does-not-trust-header-difficulty test supplies the parent's total below
    the TTD.
