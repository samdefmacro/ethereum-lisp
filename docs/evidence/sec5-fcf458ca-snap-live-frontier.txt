Root cause and repair

The asynchronous TrieNodes pipeline REFILL closure forced at least one local
work whenever STACK was non-empty, even when stack, deferred, and outstanding
remote work had exhausted the live-frontier budget. collect-missing could then
repeat local expansion during the same refill. Post-order completion barriers
and newly exposed descendants therefore had no effective stop while remote work
remained outstanding.

The repair removes that forced refill. It reserves the whole-class maximum net
expansion of one local work before entering and before every bounded-refill
iteration. The maximum is 63 works: one popped account-subtree candidate can be
replaced by the allowed 64 deferred storage dependencies. This dominates the
17-work net increase from a 16-child branch carrying both subtree and
node-completion sentinels. The ordinary non-pipeline one-item DFS escape remains
available, so an already oversized durable continuation can still drain.
